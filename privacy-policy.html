Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shohrat ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the Android game Animal Homes Puzzle (the "Game"), including its Google Play listing. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Game.

Summary

  • The Game does not ask you to create an account.
  • Puzzle progress and settings stay on your device.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise location.
  • Google Play, and advertising or diagnostic services when a build includes them, may process limited technical data under their own policies.
  • Questions and deletion requests: shohrat84@gmail.com

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Shohrat, an independent developer of games and applications.

  • Email: shohrat84@gmail.com
  • Website: https://shohrat.github.io/
  • This policy: https://shohrat.github.io/privacy-policy.html

Stored on your device

The Game saves gameplay information locally so you can continue where you left off. This can include level progress, completed puzzles, sound or other settings, and similar game state. That information remains on your device. We do not upload it to a developer server, and we do not use it to build a profile about you.

You can remove it by clearing the Game's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Information we do not collect

The Game does not require registration. We do not knowingly collect your name, email address, postal address, or phone number; precise GPS location; contacts, SMS, or call logs; photos, camera, or microphone recordings; payment card or bank account numbers; government identifiers; or an account identifier created by us.

If you email us, we receive the address you write from and the content of your message. We use that correspondence to reply and to keep a record of the request.

Google Play

When you install, update, purchase, or review the Game, Google collects and processes information under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Google account password. Through Google Play Console we may see aggregated statistics and the limited diagnostic reports Google makes available to developers, such as install counts, ratings, and crash information.

Advertising

Some builds of the Game may show advertisements so the Game can be offered free of charge. Ads are served by Google, for example through Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may process an advertising ID; an IP address, which can indicate a coarse location such as a country or city; device and app information such as device model, language, operating system, and app version; and the fact that an ad was shown or clicked.

We do not use that information to identify you by name. Google describes its advertising practices in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. If the build you installed does not include an advertising SDK, that build does not send advertising data.

Diagnostics

Google Play may collect crash and performance data through Android vitals. If a build includes an additional diagnostics tool provided by Google, that tool may receive crash logs, device model, operating system version, and app version so we can fix defects. We do not add your name or email address to those reports.

3. How we use information

We use information to run the Game and remember progress on your device, to understand crashes and improve stability, to show advertisements in builds that include them, to answer messages you send us, and to comply with the law and protect the Game, players, and us from abuse.

We do not sell personal information. We do not keep player accounts, so we do not use personal information for our own cross-app tracking.

4. When information is shared

We share information only with Google and its advertising or diagnostics services, when those services are part of the build you use; with authorities when the law, a court, or a government request requires it; and with a new developer if the Game is transferred, in which case this policy or a successor policy will describe how the Game handles information.

We do not sell or rent personal information to data brokers.

5. Children

Animal Homes Puzzle is a casual puzzle for a general audience, and families may play it. The Game does not ask players to enter a name, email address, or other contact details. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age required by your country.

If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us personal information, email shohrat84@gmail.com and we will delete it.

If the Game is included in a Google Play program for families or children, we follow that program's rules. We do not collect personal information from children through the Game. Any advertising shown in a child-directed experience uses only Google Play certified ad SDKs, is appropriate for children, and is not interest-based advertising directed at children.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device progress stays on the device until you clear the Game's data, reset progress inside the Game, or uninstall it. We cannot delete that copy from our side, because we do not keep it.

Emails you send us are kept for as long as we need to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterward in case you write again, unless you ask us to delete them and we are not required to keep them.

Data held by Google is retained under Google's own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. To ask us about deletion, email shohrat84@gmail.com. There is no in-game account to delete.

7. Security

Local game data is protected by your device and its operating system. A device lock helps if you share the phone or tablet. No storage method is perfectly secure. We do not operate a player database of names, passwords, or payment cards.

8. International processing

You may use the Game in any country where Google Play offers it. If you email us, your message may be processed where we read email. Google may process technical and advertising data in the United States and in other countries where Google operates.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can uninstall the Game or clear its storage to erase local progress, reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings, turn off ad personalization where Android provides that control, and email us to access, correct, or delete messages you sent us.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering you and keeping the Game reliable, and on the steps you request when you contact us. Gameplay data stored only on your device is not transmitted to us. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Where the CCPA or CPRA applies, we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define those terms, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

10. Permissions

The Game is designed to run without access to your contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, files outside the app, or precise location. If a build requests internet access, that access is used to reach Google Play services and, when ads are enabled, to load advertisements. You can review permissions on the Google Play listing and in Android settings.

Payments, if a build offers a paid feature, are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card number.

11. Links

The Game, its store listing, or this website may link to Google Play or to other sites we do not control. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this page when the Game's data practices or the law changes. The date at the top will change with the text. The current version is always published at https://shohrat.github.io/privacy-policy.html.
