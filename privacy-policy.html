Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Animal Homes Puzzle (the "Game"), an Android game published by Shohrat. The Game does not collect any user data.

Summary

  • Animal Homes Puzzle does not collect, transmit, or share any user data.
  • The Game has no account, no advertisements, and no analytics.
  • Puzzle progress stays on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not sell personal information. We have none to sell.
  • Questions: shohrat84@gmail.com

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Shohrat, an independent developer.

  • Game: Animal Homes Puzzle
  • Developer: Shohrat
  • Email: shohrat84@gmail.com
  • This policy: https://shohrat.github.io/privacy-policy.html

2. The Game does not collect user data

Animal Homes Puzzle does not collect any user data. It does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or enter your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information.

The Game does not collect or access:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Account identifiers, passwords, or player profiles
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, calendar, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Files outside the Game
  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers
  • App activity, gameplay events, or diagnostics sent off the device
  • Payment card or bank details

The Game does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or account services. It does not send user data to Shohrat or to third parties. It does not share user data, because it does not collect any.

If you email us, that message is separate from the Game. We receive only what you write, and we use it only to reply. The Game itself still does not collect that information.

3. Information that stays on your device

The Game may save puzzle progress and settings locally on your device so you can continue playing. That information never leaves the device. We cannot see it, copy it, or use it. Clearing the Game's storage, or uninstalling the Game, removes it.

4. Google Play

You download Animal Homes Puzzle from Google Play. Google handles your store account, installation, and updates under Google's Privacy Policy. That is Google's processing as the store, not collection by the Game. We do not receive your Google account password or a copy of your personal data from the Game.

5. Children

Animal Homes Puzzle does not collect personal information from children or from anyone else. It does not ask for a name, email address, or other contact details. If you believe a child has emailed personal information to us, write to shohrat84@gmail.com and we will delete that message.

6. Deletion

There is no account and no server copy of your gameplay. To remove progress stored on the device, clear the Game's data in Android settings or uninstall Animal Homes Puzzle.

To ask us to delete an email you sent, write to shohrat84@gmail.com. We will delete it unless we must keep it to comply with the law.

7. Security

Because the Game does not collect user data, we do not hold a database of players, names, or payment cards. Progress stored on the device is protected by your device. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

8. Your rights

You can stop using the Game and delete its local data at any time by uninstalling it or clearing its storage. Where a privacy law such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or CPRA applies, there is no user data held by the Game for us to provide, correct, or sell. We do not sell or share personal information. If you have emailed us and want that message deleted, contact shohrat84@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If the Game's data practices change, we will update this page and the date above before those practices apply. The current policy is always at https://shohrat.github.io/privacy-policy.html.
